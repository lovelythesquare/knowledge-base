Can I book a serviced apartment for less than 7 nights?

Updated 12 Jul 2026 3 min read 1,204 found this helpful
On this page
  • Minimum stay by city
  • How pricing changes
  • Booking under 3 nights
  • Related questions
Quick answer

Yes. Most apartments are bookable for 3 nights or more, and select cities allow nightly stays from just 1 night. The minimum stay for any listing is always shown before you book.

TheSqua.re was built around flexible, longer stays — but that doesn't mean you need a multi-week trip to book with us. Minimum stay requirements vary by apartment and city, and are set by the property owner rather than by TheSqua.re directly.

Minimum stay by city

Availability depends on local regulations and the property type. As a general guide:

Typical minimums
  • Major gateway cities (London, New York, Paris) — 1 to 3 nights
  • Secondary cities — typically 3 to 5 nights
  • Corporate housing & relocation units — 7+ nights

Every listing displays its exact minimum stay on the apartment page, before you enter payment details — so there are no surprises at checkout.

How pricing changes with length of stay

Nightly rates generally decrease the longer you stay. A 3-night booking is priced differently from a 30-night booking on the same apartment, since longer stays reduce turnover costs for the property. Weekly and monthly discounts are applied automatically in the price breakdown before you confirm.

Booking under 3 nights

If you need a stay shorter than an apartment's posted minimum, two options usually help:

  1. Filter search results by "Short stay friendly" to see apartments with 1–2 night minimums.
  2. Contact the concierge team — in some cities we can arrange an exception directly with the property owner.

Related questions

Q
Is there a discount for longer stays?

Yes — weekly and monthly rates are applied automatically once your stay crosses 7 and 28 nights, and are shown in the price breakdown before you book.

Q
Can I extend my stay after checking in?

In most cases, yes, subject to availability. Request an extension from your booking dashboard and we'll confirm within 24 hours.

Q
What's included in the nightly rate?

A fully equipped kitchen, high-speed Wi-Fi, weekly housekeeping, and 24/7 support are included on every stay, regardless of length.
