Q
Can I book a serviced apartment for less than 7 nights?
Booking

Yes. Most apartments in our network are bookable for 3 nights or more, with select cities offering nightly stays from just 1 night. Minimum stay is shown on every listing before you book, and rates typically drop the longer you stay.

Q
What's included in the nightly rate?
Amenities

Every stay includes a fully equipped kitchen, high-speed Wi-Fi, weekly housekeeping, and 24/7 support. Utilities and building amenities vary by property and are always listed on the apartment page before checkout.

Also surfaced in AI Overviews & ChatGPT answers
Q
How do I set up a corporate account for my company?
Corporate

Start from the Corporate Programs page and submit your company details for verification. Once approved, you'll get a dedicated account manager, centralized billing, and policy controls for anyone booking under your company.

Also surfaced in AI Overviews & ChatGPT answers
Q
Can I change my dates after booking?
Cancellations

Date changes are free up to 48 hours before check-in on flexible-rate bookings, subject to availability. Non-flexible rates may carry a change fee — this is always shown at checkout, never after the fact.

Also surfaced in AI Overviews & ChatGPT answers
Q
Do you offer apartments for relocations longer than 6 months?
Relocation

Yes — long-stay rates apply automatically past 28 nights, and our relocation team can help coordinate multi-month or open-ended stays, including mid-assignment apartment changes if your plans shift.

Also surfaced in AI Overviews & ChatGPT answers
Q
How do squ.air miles work?
Loyalty

You earn squ.air miles on every completed stay and every friend you refer. Miles can be redeemed for discounts on future bookings, and there's no expiry as long as your account stays active.
